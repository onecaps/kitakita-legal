Common questions

How do I stop sharing my location?

Open Settings → Privacy and turn off Share with circles. You can also leave a circle at any time — once you leave, the other members stop seeing your position. Sharing is always limited to circles you have joined; KitaKita never shares your location publicly.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete Account and confirm. This removes your profile, including your medical details, and signs you out everywhere. It cannot be undone.

If you can no longer sign in and need your account removed, email us from the address on the account and we will handle it.

Why does KitaKita ask for background location?

Live location and arrival alerts are the core of the app — your family only sees where you are, and only gets a "got home safe" notification, if the app can update your position while it is closed. You are asked before the permission prompt appears, and you can revoke it at any time in iOS Settings.

Continued use of GPS running in the background can dramatically decrease battery life.

Someone in a chat is being abusive.

In a one-to-one chat, tap the … button at the top right and choose Block to stop their messages, or Report to tell us what happened. Blocking is reversible — the same menu offers Unblock afterwards.

In a group chat there is no single person to block, so leave the group, or ask whoever runs it to remove them.

For anything serious, email ecapsdev@gmail.com with a screenshot and the name of the person involved. Abuse reports are handled ahead of everything else in the queue.

My family member's location looks stuck or out of date.

Each person's marker shows how recently it updated. A stale marker usually means their phone lost signal, ran out of battery, or has location permission turned off or set to "While Using the App". Ask them to open KitaKita and check Settings → Privacy.

What is "Ask KitaKita"?

It is a built-in assistant that answers weather and safety questions in plain language, grounded in a live forecast for your area. It appears as a pinned chat thread. It is software, not a person, and it is not a substitute for emergency services.

Is SOS a replacement for calling 911?

No. SOS alerts the people in your circles and attaches your location. In a real emergency, call the official hotlines first — the Emergency tab lists them for your area.
